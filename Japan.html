🧭 - 8 Day Tokyo Itinerary

Tap a day to peek inside ✨

Day 1 — Shinjuku (Tue) +
  • Land in Tokyo; pick up Suica/PASMO
  • Take the metro to Airbnb in Shinjuku
  • Check in and settle
  • Dinner nearby & explore Omoide Yokocho / neon streets

Some Golden Gai bars have a cover charge—carry a little cash.

Day 2 — Harajuku & Shibuya (Wed) +
  • Hachiko statue → cross Shibuya Crossing
  • Don Quijote + arcade buildings
  • Walk to Harajuku (Takeshita Street)
  • Yoyogi Park → Meiji Jingu
  • Shibuya Sky (book ahead; go for sunset views)

Prime Shibuya Sky slots sell out—reserve early.

Day 3 — Asakusa, Akihabara & Ueno (Thu) +
  • Be ready early for Senso-ji & Kaminarimon Gate
  • Five-story Pagoda → Nakamise Street
  • Asakusa Culture Info Center (8F view)
  • Hoppy Street → Denbouin-dori snacks
  • Akihabara: Radio Kaikan & GiGO arcades
  • Ueno: Museum of Nature & Science, Inari Shrine

Arriving early at Senso-ji means softer light and fewer crowds.

Day 4 — Tokyo DisneySea (Fri) +
  • DisneySea adventures, snacks, parades, and shows

Use the official app for wait times and mobile orders.

Day 5 — Tsukiji & Ginza (Sat) +
  • Tsukiji Outer Market: fish stalls & bites
  • Ramen Takahashi (or a local favorite)
  • Ginza Six → Uniqlo flagship (12F) → Itoya stationery

Go early to Tsukiji for the freshest samples.

Day 6 — teamLab Planets & Mario Kart (Sun) +
  • teamLab Planets (barefoot digital art)
  • Mario Kart street drive (license/permit as needed)

Wear shorts or pants you can roll up—some exhibits involve water.

Day 7 — Day Trip to Kamakura (Mon) +
  • Great Buddha (Kotoku-in) & Hasedera
  • Komachi-dori snacks → Enoden seaside ride

Grab an Enoden pass if you’ll hop along the coast.

Day 8 — Chill Morning & Departure (Tue) +
  • Easy breakfast & last-minute shopping
  • Head to the airport ✈️

Top up Suica/PASMO before you go—keep it for next time!
